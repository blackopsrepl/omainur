# 008 ear-on-artifact: the render carries the identity audibly

## Verdict: VALIDATED (castle, seed 1986)

### Method
- Soloed the lead carrier (mute every other track — data-level, engine-honored)
  and measured per-section chroma of the RENDER; correlated each section
  against A (the call) as cosine on 12-bin pitch-class mass, octave-blind by
  construction.
- Two experiments, one finding: at FULL-MIX level F scores 0.50 against A
  because its backing harmony re-lands the hook on the b-progression —
  identity is masked by color at mix level. On the soloed carrier F
  corroborates at 0.70. Both numbers are correct; they measure different
  things. The gate uses the carrier (what recurrence claims) and the mix
  (what a listener hears) as separate, named lenses.

### Result
- **GA corroboration: 5/5** lane-claimed hook statements audible in the
  artifact (threshold 0.60): B 0.907, C 1.000, D 0.903, E 0.994, F 0.703,
  G 0.604. The register-shifted answers (B/D tails +12) corroborate —
  pitch-class chroma is octave-blind exactly as the design required.
- **GB contrast: no flat sections** (spread floor 0.02; worst 0.14).
- The pack ceiling does NOT hide hook recurrence on the artifact. The
  instrument-envelope escalation flag stays down.

### Negative control on the method itself
- First run sliced E–H from a 14-bar render: empty audio, spreads 0.0,
  GA failed on 1/4. Fix: render 33 bars. An empty section's flat chroma is a
  spike bug, never engine evidence — gate the slice coverage before scoring.
